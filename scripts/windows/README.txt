telemeSYNC - telemetry overlay generator for bike videos
========================================================

HOW TO START
1. Extract this ZIP (right-click > "Extract All...") to a folder, for example Documents.
2. Open the "telemeSYNC" folder and double-click "Iniciar telemeSYNC.bat" (Start telemeSYNC).
3. The browser opens at http://localhost:5174. Keep the black window open while you use the app.

There is nothing to install: everything the app needs is already in this folder.
The language is chosen with the flags at the top right of the app (English by default).
Full instructions with pictures of the Windows warnings (in Portuguese): Instrucoes-telemeSYNC.pdf

IF WINDOWS SHOWS "Windows protected your PC"
Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

TO STOP
Close the black window.

Your videos never leave your computer: the app only reads their metadata (date, length, fps).
